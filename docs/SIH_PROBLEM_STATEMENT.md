SIH26133 — Problem Statement

Accessibility and Quality of Public Healthcare Services, Particularly in Rural and Underserved Areas

Develop a software solution to improve the accessibility and quality of public healthcare services, particularly for people living in rural and underserved areas.

The solution should address challenges such as limited access to specialist doctors, long travel distances, overcrowding and inefficient appointment or queue management, delayed referrals, fragmented patient records, irregular access to diagnostic services, difficulty tracking high-risk patients, limited visibility of medicine availability, and poor coordination between different levels of healthcare facilities.

The proposed solution should facilitate better coordination between patients, healthcare workers, primary and secondary healthcare facilities, specialists, diagnostic services, pharmacies, and healthcare administrators.

The system is expected to support areas such as:

- Teleconsultation and access to specialist healthcare.
- Appointment and queue management.
- Digital patient records and longitudinal health records.
- Digital triage and identification of high-risk patients.
- Referral management and referral tracking between healthcare facilities.
- Diagnostic test coordination and access to diagnostic reports.
- Medicine availability and inventory visibility.
- Follow-up and monitoring of high-risk and chronic-care patients.
- Healthcare facility dashboards for monitoring healthcare service delivery and operational indicators.
- Multilingual and accessible interfaces suitable for rural populations.
- Functionality suitable for areas with limited or intermittent internet connectivity.

The overall objective is to reduce barriers to accessing public healthcare, improve continuity and coordination of care, reduce unnecessary travel and delays, and improve the delivery and quality of healthcare services in rural and underserved communities.
